● You're right — on single-choice nodes, it's just a "skip the countdown"
   button. The current model requires every node to have a default choice
   for auto-advance to work, so single-choice nodes end up with a
  meaningless Continue button + 8-second countdown every transition.
  That's a lot of friction for what is conceptually just "play the next
  segment."

  Where Continue does earn its keep is on multi-choice nodes (like
  chapter-1's "Deep dive vs Continue") — there it's the explicit "stay on
   the main line" option.

  The right behavior is probably: if a node has exactly one choice and
  it's the default, skip the choice UI entirely and auto-advance
  immediately. No button, no countdown, just a clean cut to the next
  segment. Multi-choice nodes still show the UI as today.

  Want me to wire that in? It'd be a 3-line change in onSegmentEnded.
  Side effect: your intro → chapter-1 and chapter-2 → aside-default
  transitions become seamless, but chapter-1's "Deep dive vs Continue"
